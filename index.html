Questions

What haulage offices ask before they move off a whiteboard and a spreadsheet.

Email a question

Can I bring my old jobs in?

Yes. Import a CSV from Mandata or a spreadsheet: 14,109 real jobs took about 45 seconds, and an import can be undone.

Can I look round first?

Yes. Open the sample company from the sign-in screen: six months of a haulier's work on every page, with no sign-up.

Does it export to Sage 50?

Yes: invoices, credit notes and payments. Other accounts packages are not supported yet.

Does it suit a one-man band?

Yes. Switch on one-man band and the office login is also the driver, with the phone screens inside the app.

Can a planner see the company's money?

Only if you tick it. Each login sees the sections you give it, and the change takes effect on their next click.
