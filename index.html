How a data point becomes a finding

One example, followed through all six steps.

A tipper carries 20 tonnes from a quarry to a housing site, and does the same run most days.

  1. Collect

    Records arrive from every part of the system, and from your tracker, fuel cards and payroll.

    ExampleThe job at £6.50 a tonne. The tracker: in at 07:12, out at 08:05.

  2. Match

    Each record is pinned to the load it belongs to: the site visit, the vehicle's fuel that month, the driver's pay that day.

    ExampleThe 53-minute stop sits inside the site's boundary, so it's this load's tip.

  3. Real cost

    The quoted cost is set beside what the load really cost, with its share of the empty miles.

    ExampleQuoted at £71. It really cost £84, mostly in waiting.

  4. Like for like

    It's compared only with work of the same kind: the same vehicle type, a similar distance, the same kind of day.

    ExampleYour other sites hold a tipper for 18 minutes on average.

  5. Rule out luck

    The pattern has to hold across many days, and the bar rises with the number of checks run, so chance doesn't produce a finding.

    Example212 visits over six months. The gap holds across them.

  6. Say it plainly

    It's written up in plain English, with the pounds, how sure it is, what to do, and the records behind it.

    Example"35 minutes longer, about £5,900 a quarter. Charge waiting after 30 minutes."

Questions

What haulage offices ask first.

Can I bring my old jobs in?

Yes. Import from Mandata or a spreadsheet: 14,109 real jobs took about 45 seconds, and an import can be undone.

Can I look round first?

Yes. Open the sample company from the sign-in screen: six months of a haulier's work on every page, with no sign-up.

Does it export to Sage 50?

Yes: invoices, credit notes and payments, with haulage and workshop each to its own nominal code.

Does it suit a one-man band?

Yes. Switch on one-man band and your office login is also the driver, with the phone screens inside the app.

Can a planner see the company's money?

Only if you tick it. Each login sees just the sections you give it, and a change takes effect on their next click.

How much does it cost?

One price per vehicle a month, with every login included. Prices are on request, so ask us and we'll give you a figure for your fleet.

Can it price per tonne?

Yes, and per load, unit, mile, hour or day, with a price list per customer. Waiting time and a fuel surcharge can be added on.

Where is my data kept?

In the UK, on servers in London. Each company's data is kept apart from every other's, and you can download all of it at any time.
