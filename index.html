How it's checked

Proper statistics, not guesswork

The MOT plan

Your MOTs planned around the rules, so you never lose a month.

Tell it your test days, such as every Friday, one booking a day. It places every vehicle and trailer on the best day.

  • Gains you a month where the rules allow it, and never loses one without asking.
  • Keeps a vehicle and its trailer together as one booking, and suggests trailer swaps that save a trip.
  • Every date checked with DVSA, trailers included, then the plan exports to Excel.

Questions

What haulage offices ask first.

Can I bring my old jobs in?

Yes. Import from Mandata or a spreadsheet: 14,109 real jobs took about 45 seconds, and an import can be undone.

Can I look round first?

Yes. Open the sample company from the sign-in screen: six months of a haulier's work on every page, with no sign-up.

Does it export to Sage 50?

Yes: invoices, credit notes and payments, with haulage and workshop each to its own nominal code.

Does it suit a one-man band?

Yes. Switch on one-man band and your office login is also the driver, with the phone screens inside the app.

Can a planner see the company's money?

Only if you tick it. Each login sees just the sections you give it, and a change takes effect on their next click.

Where is my data kept?

In the UK, on servers in London. Each company's data is kept apart from every other's, and you can download all of it at any time.
